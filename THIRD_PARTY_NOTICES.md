# Third-party sources and licences

This library includes the style descriptions, case notes and text source of [Lemo-Opuscar](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar), pinned to version `413a6bd6d78ebd3bdd5abaec9538a26867f890df`.

The source software and documentation were published by LemoLab in 2026 under the MIT License. The full copyright and licence notice is kept in [vendor/lemo-opuscar/LICENSE](vendor/lemo-opuscar/LICENSE) and ships with every source style pack. The imported files and their hashes are in [manifest.json](vendor/lemo-opuscar/manifest.json).

The source text, cases and code stay traceable; the Chinese adaptation, the metadata interface, inheritance and validation are maintained by this project. The original author's brand, experience, claims about model capability and finished-film acceptance are not this project's results.

The gallery's 59 key frames and 59 sample clips come from LemoLab's Lemo-Opuscar, and the films and stills are published under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The key-frame manifest is [reference-frames.json](assets/gallery/reference-frames.json) and the sample manifest is [samples.json](assets/gallery/samples.json). The latter records the web Release assets, the hashes of the original files and of the distributed files, and the changes made; each case's third-party material credits stay in its own demo/CREDITS.

56 sample clips are included unchanged; game-show, paper-lantern and news-package had their audio tracks removed with the picture unchanged, and the voice recordings excluded there do not ship with this library. The original sound sources, fonts, models and datasets are not included separately.
