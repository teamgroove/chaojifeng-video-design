# From plan to finished film


1. **Settle the through-line and the directorial intent.** Make clear the viewer's starting point/end point, the spine, the structural reason, the real basis and the identity that must be kept. Define the mission of the person, product, object or concept, and plan sound and energy rise and fall in advance; "tech feel" has to land in content-related visual and auditory choices. Simple films write plan.design.rationale, complex films use the optional plan.director and the [director template](../templates/director.json); expand the [direction template](../templates/direction.md) as needed. Do not copy a reference author's history or treat sample data as fact.
2. **Write shot tasks and an editable plan.** Use the [plan contract](plan-contract.md) and the [minimal template](../templates/plan.json). Every shot states the information/feeling it adds, the focus, the reveal order, the shot purpose, the handoff to the next shot and the stable dwell; this can be recorded in scene.intent. When you need to box an interface, explain an operation or highlight evidence, read [Visual emphasis and dynamic annotation](visual-emphasis.md): define the target and the protected areas first, then arrange labels, leader lines, local focus and entrances/exits; camera and annotation share the correct mapping, and must not cover subtitles or key content. Time uses absolute seconds of the whole film; shots/objects/beats/voiceover/assets have stable IDs. Product and knowledge films do not force character conflict; music films do not force three acts.
3. **Land assets and time.** Prefer user assets and real product/data evidence; when a factual picture lacks assets, search the original source online first, record candidates, rights, reasons for choosing/excluding and stopping conditions, then add charts or non-evidence generated images. Plan layers, the main action and the cross-shot handoff by shot mission; reuse an existing executor that can do the job. Code-drawn and procedural sound effects likewise keep their implementation. Models, generators, downloaded content and asset packs must not be concealed; when the user restricts to a single model, hold that boundary strictly.
4. **Rehearse sound and picture together.** Per [Sound and rhythm](sound-and-rhythm.md) settle the lead sound, the motif, the rise and fall, and the silence. When choosing a score read [Music direction and segment selection](music-direction.md): write each section's emotion and responsibility first, then find candidate segments in the [music catalogue](../assets/music/index.html) or with a query script, recording the original track time and the landing point in the finished film; you may run one track throughout, split the same track, or combine across tracks, but do not default to looping the whole song. Distinguish measured, directorial inference and already auditioned; verify assets and the scope of use first. With voiceover, obtain the real audio and measure per-sentence duration before laying out visual beats; estimates may only be marked estimated. Link picture and sound with beat_id; do not claim automatic word-level alignment. With many sound effects write the [event map](../templates/audio-event-map.json) first (scheduled moment = action moment + onset offset, generated from the same data), regenerate it after any re-lay-out of time, and after rendering hand the music-free SFX stem to `audio_event_audit.py` to check; for the method see [Launch-film grammar · Sound](launch-film-grammar.md#3-sound-write-the-event-map-first-then-lay-out-the-timeline). When the user asks for sound, the auditory plan and the actual audio track are both part of the delivery.
5. **Finish production on the agreed cadence.** direct generates the complete editable source and the finished film straight away, checking and fixing the hard sections within the same round, without interrupting for a test cut or a direction confirmation; other tasks may first use keyframes/short segments/rough cut to test the through-line, the reading window, the sound-image landing points and the seams, then continue to completion, and wait for confirmation only when the user asks for staged. Precise storyboarding or bounded exploration is allowed; keep alternative expressions and stopping conditions for failing shots. Look at the first frame, the densest frame, the relationship-lands frame, the transition boundaries and continuous playback. Claim a uniform clock and random seek only when they are measured to be consistent. Look up concrete APIs in the current documentation; do not assemble parameters from memory.
6. **Review, export, deliver.** Do the full render that is required and save the sources, the video and the [delivery record](../templates/delivery.json). Per [Review and revision](review-and-revision.md) actually check sound, picture, aesthetics, rhythm, narrative and tech feel, and separately check facts, playback and artifact consistency. Fix known wrong words, obvious subtitle misalignment or long waits that do not match the task first; do not substitute disclosing a defect for meeting a necessary requirement; mark unresolved versions explicitly as candidate/not passed. Record the current plan and the file hashes; items not watched, not listened to and not audience-tested stay unverified; do not guarantee aesthetic results or improved comprehension.
